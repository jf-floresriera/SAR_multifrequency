## Introduction
- The 24 June 2026 Mw 7.2 + 7.5 doublet, San Sebastián–Boconó fault system, damage in La Guaira.
- Rapid products already released (ESA, NASA/JPL NISAR UR, JAXA, USGS, Scripps/UCSD, LUZ GNSS+DInSAR).
- Gap: no study combines C-, L- and X-band SAR and optical data quantitatively from fault to building scale.
- Objectives and outline.

## Data
- Sentinel-1 (C-band), NISAR (L-band), ALOS-2 (L-band), Umbra (X-band), Planet (optical), GNSS, ancillary (see Data and Resources). → Table 1, Figure 1, Figure 2.

## Methods
- Interferometric processing and harmonisation (single LOS sign convention, coherence mask, ionospheric correction, common grid). → Table 2.
- East/Up decomposition per band and GNSS validation.
- Damage analysis: amplitude, texture, normalised difference look index (NDLI), optical comparison.

## Results
- Coseismic LOS and East/Up in C- and L-band (Figures 3–5).

- Building-scale damage (Figure 8; Table 3); damage vs deformation gradient (Figure 9); infrastructure (Figure 10).
